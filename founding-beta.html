Train Medusa with the judgement only your industry experience can provide.

Medusa Heads is not live yet. We are selecting the selected early participants to help us test whether a knowledgeable person can train a Head using their own industry judgement while we supervise the journey, answer questions about the system and learn where the training path needs to improve.

300 VS 300 / OPTIONAL PERFORMANCE PROGRAMME
Qualifying participants can receive 25% off their first 300 Medusa opportunities when they opt into the 300 vs 300 comparison.
The £19.99 one-off Head purchase remains separate. The comparison discount applies only to the first 300 Medusa opportunities, subject to programme acceptance and completion requirements. Subscriptions and additional Coins are priced separately.

How the beta works: you do not need to become a Medusa expert, and we do not need to become experts in your industry. You bring the commercial knowledge; Medusa asks for the judgement behind it. We supervise the process so that if a question feels unclear, irrelevant or incomplete, we can learn from that and improve the training path.
What we are testing: can someone who knows their market train a useful Head without us knowing that market first? During the beta, we will supervise rather than lead. If Medusa asks the wrong question, misses an important branch, or makes you force your knowledge into the wrong box, tell us. That friction is part of what we want to learn.
What happens next: this is an application to the optional comparison programme, not the start of live training. We use your answers to understand the judgement you would bring to Medusa and to select a varied first beta group. Accepted clients will be invited into supervised training when the beta opens.
How the beta is measured: the optional 60-day outreach comparison is a controlled 300 / 300 comparison. You provide or generate 300 opportunities using your existing method. Medusa independently produces 300 opportunities against the same commercial objective and agreed criteria. Wherever practical, both groups then go through the same downstream process so we can compare fit, research effort, response, meetings, disqualifications and commercial outcomes fairly.
