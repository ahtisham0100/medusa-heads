06 / SIMPLE RULES

NO CREDIT
MATHEMATICS.

One Coin always means one newly delivered qualified opportunity. The subscription changes what happens around that opportunity, not the meaning of the Coin.

Do better plans get better research?

Same evidence standard, more selective options. Seek can select up to the top 40% of scanned organisations, Enrich as tightly as the top 30%, and Pursue as tightly as the top 20%. These are ceilings, not delivery guarantees: an organisation must still clear the qualification standard. Each delivery shows how many organisations were scanned.

What if Medusa finds a duplicate?

A repeat organisation does not consume another Coin. Neither does a rejected result.

Can I top up without changing plan?

Yes. Add a Purse whenever you need more volume. One-off Purses roll over for 90 days.

What happens when a niche runs dry?

Release the next band from the Head’s Reserve, or let it move to Monitoring and report only new entrants and changes. Add another Head to open a new market.

Can I change selection later?

Yes, per Head, at any time. Loosen it freely; tighten it to your plan’s limit. Upgrading unlocks tighter selection.

Does the Reserve cost extra?

No. Holding it is included. A released opportunity is re-checked, then uses one Coin like any other delivery.

Do I need an outreach platform?

Only for Pursue. Connect the account you already use, or open one with a supported provider; they bill you directly.

How are Cerberus checks charged?

Premium company verification is included. Cerberus adds people-level verification at £0.30 per delivered Coin. Cerberus Pack sits on top of Cerberus and adds deeper financial verification using relevant paid-data sources for a further £0.50 per delivered Coin, making £0.80 total for a Pack-checked Coin.
